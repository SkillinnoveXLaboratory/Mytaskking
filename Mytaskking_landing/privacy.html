Legal and privacy

Privacy Policy

How MyTaskKing collects, uses, protects, and shares information when organizations and their authorized users use our workforce collaboration platform.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 | Version 1.0.0

In short: MyTaskKing is an organization-managed workspace. We process information needed to provide chat, tasks, meetings, calls, attendance, telecaller CRM, field-force tools, work activity features, and support. Access to organization data is controlled by roles and organization settings.

1. Scope and who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to MyTaskKing mobile applications, desktop applications, websites, and related services (collectively, the "Services"). MyTaskKing is a collaboration and workforce-management platform used by organizations and their authorized workers, managers, field executives, telecallers, clients, and administrators. This policy explains our handling of information associated with people who use the Services or whose information is provided by an organization using the Services.

When your employer, client organization, or another organization provides you with a MyTaskKing account, that organization is generally responsible for deciding which features are enabled, who can access its workspace, and how long it keeps organization content. We process organization data to provide the Services and according to the organization administrator's instructions, applicable agreements, and applicable law.

2. Information we collect

We collect information that is necessary to create and protect accounts, provide requested features, operate organization workspaces, and improve reliability. The information collected depends on your role, device, permissions, and the features your organization enables.

  • Account and profile information: tenant or organization name, user ID, name, role, profile details, password credentials, presence status, and account settings. MyTaskKing uses organization tenant, user ID, and password authentication rather than public employee self-signup.
  • Workspace content: messages, channel memberships, task titles and comments, files, images, voice notes, calendar items, meeting information, call history, reactions, saved items, and other content you or your organization submit through the Services.
  • Operational and employment information: attendance clock-in and clock-out records, leave requests, task assignments, reports, workday status, and role-specific workflow records.
  • Sales and field-force information: leads, call outcomes, follow-up notes, customer or outlet records, product and order details, visit records, and field activity submitted by authorized users.
  • Location information: approximate or precise location where you grant permission or use a location-based feature, such as attendance, field visits, outlet navigation, route activity, or best-effort desktop login location. Location collection depends on device permissions and enabled organization features.
  • Device and security information: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, login activity, session identifiers, user-agent information, timestamps, and security or audit events used to maintain account security and investigate misuse.
  • Optional sensitive inputs: mobile selfie verification, microphone access for calls or voice notes, camera access for approved captures, and support-ticket screenshots when you choose or are asked to provide them for a feature.
  • Desktop activity information: where enabled by an organization, the desktop agent may create periodic work-activity screenshots and related timestamps. The application is designed to show a capture indicator and log capture events for administrator review.

3. How we use information

We use information to deliver the features requested by users and organizations: authenticate accounts; maintain tenant-separated workspaces; send messages and notifications; create tasks and reports; run calls and meetings; support attendance, field, telecaller, and client-access workflows; show relevant dashboards; and provide customer support. We also use limited technical and security information to prevent fraud, detect unauthorized access, troubleshoot failures, enforce permissions, maintain backups, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information or sensitive personal information. We do not use workspace content for unrelated advertising. We do not use permissions for purposes that are not connected to the feature for which they are requested. If a feature requires sensitive access that may not be expected, such as location, camera, microphone, or desktop capture, the Service should request the relevant device permission in context and describe the purpose before use.

4. How information is shared

MyTaskKing shares information only as needed to operate the Services and as directed by the organization using them. Your information may be visible to other authorized users in your organization's workspace, for example channel members, task assignees, managers, administrators, or field-team supervisors. Client users are restricted to the channels and access periods assigned to them.

We may use service providers that support hosting, file delivery, notifications, communications, and business search capabilities. Depending on the enabled feature, these providers may include cloud storage or file-delivery services, media-storage services, push-notification providers, realtime communication providers, mapping or location providers, and external business-data services used for shop search. These providers process information only to provide the applicable service. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the Services, or during a business transfer permitted by law.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Services, meet contractual responsibilities to an organization, resolve disputes, maintain security records, and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods can vary by data type and organization configuration. For example, a workspace may retain messages, tasks, field records, or audit logs according to its operational requirements, while temporary session data may expire sooner.

Authorized users may update certain profile information through the Services. To request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal information, first contact your organization administrator because the organization controls its workspace data. You may also contact MyTaskKing through the privacy contact listed below. We will evaluate requests in accordance with applicable law and our obligations to the organization. Account deletion requests may require removal or anonymization of associated data, subject to legal, security, backup, and record-retention requirements.

6. Security and access controls

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include role-based access control, tenant-based data scoping, session management, audit logging, authentication controls, and access reviews. No system can guarantee absolute security, so users should protect their credentials, use trusted devices, and report suspected account compromise promptly.

7. Your choices and permissions

You can control many permissions through your device settings. Declining a permission may limit related features, such as location-based attendance, camera-based verification, microphone-enabled calling, or field visit capture. Organization administrators may also control feature availability, client access windows, work-activity intervals, and other workspace settings. If you believe your account access or data is incorrect, contact your organization administrator or our privacy contact.

8. Children, international use, and changes

MyTaskKing is designed for business and organization use and is not directed to children. Do not use the Services if you are not permitted to do so under applicable law or your organization's policies. Information may be processed in locations where MyTaskKing, an organization, or our service providers operate. We may update this policy when our Services, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated version will be posted at this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact your organization administrator first. You can also contact the MyTaskKing privacy team at privacy@mytaskking.com. Please include your organization name, your user ID, and a clear description of your request so we can respond appropriately.

Important: This page is designed to transparently describe the product features documented for MyTaskKing. Before publishing it as the final legal policy or submitting it to an app store, have the policy, the listed privacy contact, actual data flows, retention settings, and the store Data Safety declaration reviewed by qualified legal and privacy professionals.
