How it works

A clearer way to run the workday.

MyTaskKing gives each person the tools they need, while keeping every conversation and outcome connected.

Enter your workspace

Sign in securely with your organization’s tenant, user ID, and password. Every organization stays in its own protected workspace.

Plan and coordinate

Chat in channels, assign tasks, run meetings, share files, and keep deadlines visible without switching tools.

See work move forward

Managers get live activity, field visits, attendance, reports, and role-aware dashboards to make timely decisions.
